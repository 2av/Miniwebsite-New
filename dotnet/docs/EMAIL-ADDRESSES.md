# ShareBizz mailbox rules

Use these addresses when sending or receiving ShareBizz mail. Do not send customer, partner, billing, or recruitment mail from a personal inbox.

Current code still sends from `support@miniwebsite.in`. New and updated mail must follow this document.

## Addresses

| Address | Purpose | Extra rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `noreply@sharebizz.com` | OTP, security, and other system notifications | Set `Reply-To` to a monitored mailbox. The customer's Reply button must not land on `noreply`. |
| `support@sharebizz.com` | Customer and partner support | Shared mailbox or ticketing system. Do not depend on one employee. |
| `business@sharebizz.com` | Business enquiries, partnerships, and acquisition | Keep commercial mail out of the support queue. |
| `billing@sharebizz.com` | Payments, invoices, refunds | Financial queries only. |
| `marketing@sharebizz.com` | Promotional campaigns | Do not use `noreply` as the campaign sender. |
| `careers@sharebizz.com` | Job applications | Send an auto-reply. |
| `hr@sharebizz.com` | Confidential employee matters | Not a public support address. |

Do not create `sales@`, `partners@`, `franchise@`, or `investor@` yet. Those conversations use `business@sharebizz.com`.

## noreply@sharebizz.com

Automated mail only. The sender does not expect a reply, and this address is not a support inbox.

Use it for:

- Registration OTP and email verification
- Welcome mail and login credentials, where those are sent
- Password reset and security alerts
- Order and payment confirmation, and invoice notification
- Profile activation and renewal reminders
- Partner membership and account-status notifications

Set `Reply-To` to the mailbox that should receive the reply:

- Account, profile, partner, or general questions: `support@sharebizz.com`
- Payment, invoice, or refund questions: `billing@sharebizz.com`

Do not use `noreply` as the primary sender for bulk promotional campaigns.

## support@sharebizz.com

Use this wherever a customer or partner should get a reply from a person.

- Interested-partner questions, onboarding help, and existing partner support
- Login, account, and Digital Business Profile problems
- FRP, DMP, PSP, SMP, and FSP support
- Commission, referral, and wallet queries
- Complaints, corrections, and escalations

## business@sharebizz.com

Company-level and commercial mail. Use it to reach potential Franchisee Partners, Digital Marketing Partners, Print Shop Partners, and Field Sales Partners, and for proposals and partnership discussions.

- Business enquiries and potential partnerships
- Franchise and investor enquiries
- Vendors, service providers, and strategic collaborations
- Explaining ShareBizz features and benefits to small businesses
- Approved offers and promotional campaigns
- Product updates, webinars, and partner training invitations
- Follow-up campaigns to interested prospects, with consent and an unsubscribe path

## billing@sharebizz.com

- Customer or partner asks for an invoice or receipt
- Payment is successful, failed, or pending
- GST invoice correction
- Refund or payment-reversal status
- Digital Business Profile renewal or partner membership fee
- Payment amount does not match the invoice

## careers@sharebizz.com

- Job openings and applications
- Interview scheduling
- Interview updates and selection or rejection mail
- Resume and candidate follow-up
- Auto-reply when an application arrives

## hr@sharebizz.com

- Employee onboarding and document requests
- Leave, attendance, and HR queries
- Policies and internal announcements
- Other employment-related communication
